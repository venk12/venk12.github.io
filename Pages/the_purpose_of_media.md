
# The Purpose of Media

![Media image](https://d2w9rnfcy7mm78.cloudfront.net/15355627/original_46684b8ba1592b4ada7eb6d26b4e2a72.png)

I am no media tycoon. At a human level, media should be aimed at creating and transmitting accurate information to people.

Today, media includes television, social apps, and video platforms. How people consume information has changed drastically. Until the 1980s, many people consumed information through regional and national newspapers and radio channels and had limited access to global news. The new world of technology promised a better informed world, but in many ways it has become noisier: entertainment and attention capture often overshadow accurate reporting.

We can't blame media alone; it also reflects us. We seek entertainment, and boredom is an increasing problem in stable societies. Boredom is one of the challenges of the 21st century.
