
# The Purpose of Media

I am no media tycoon.But at a human level, media should be aimed at creating and transmitting the most accurate information to people.

Media today is so many things - television channels, social media apps, youtube. How people consume information has changed drastically. Until the 80s, my parents used to consume information mostly through regional and national newspapers and radio channels. They had limited information available about the world. The brave new world of technology promised a better world. But instead of a better world, what we have instead going after is a more noisy world. Noise is everywhere, our media leads us from one entertaining news to another. The news we see is not the actual news but a version that glues us to the media transmitting it. The good old news of making your think it's no more. 

But we can't blame it entirely on media for all the mishaps, it's inherent a reflection of ourselves. We need entertainment from our leaders because we are deeply bored. We don't know what to do with our lives. Many developed countries have so much stability that people are starting to lose a sense of purpose. Boredom is the disease of 21st century.
