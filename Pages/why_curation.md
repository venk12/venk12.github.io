# Why curation is important?

Curation reflects a lived experience. 

It was curated because the person who curated it not only wanted to speak information - because information is widespread today. Its also because they had a genuine interest in letting another person know how it feels to be them, to think like them, to be in the moment. So in a way, curation is about teleporting experiences.

I was born in India.When I was 13 years old,I was lucky to have access to a computer. My teacher at school taught be BASIC programming language and HTML during that time. I remember sitting in front of the computer after school hours to make my first HTML website. 

> The coolest feature at the time was Marque, which makes the text move across the screen.

Not so surprisingly, my first website was about clothes and fashion around the world. This inclination to catalog everything I see, I know has been natural. I don't know why I did it. Maybe it started with a feeling that it makes me look knowledgeable. But retrospectively, I would done that even if no body was reading it. It was just intrinsic drive to collect a body of knowledge for the sheer joy of it. It might help people, but it more importantly refines my thinking. 

I decided I want to bring it back to life. So I am going back to my old ways building a simple HTML blog, freely hosted on GitHub, can be maintained without a lot of overhead, editable from any device with an internet. Designed for personal curation and personal consumption. Not for any audience in particular. Not with an intent to make it viral - but to leave a small footprint of my digital and real life out there for the world to consume.
