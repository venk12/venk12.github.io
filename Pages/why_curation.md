# Why curation is important

Curation reflects a lived experience. It is more than sharing information—it's an effort to convey how something feels to the curator, to teleport an experience in a compact form.

I was born in India. When I was 13 I was lucky to have access to a computer. My teacher taught BASIC and HTML, and I remember sitting after school to make my first website.

> The coolest feature at the time was the marquee tag, which made text move across the screen.

Not surprisingly, my first website was about clothes and fashion around the world. This inclination to catalogue what I see has felt natural. I did it because it was intrinsically enjoyable and it helped clarify my thinking. It might help others, but more importantly it refines my own ideas.

I decided to bring that habit back to life with a simple HTML blog, hosted on GitHub and easy to maintain from any device. It is designed for personal curation and reflection—not to go viral, but to leave a small footprint of life and ideas.
